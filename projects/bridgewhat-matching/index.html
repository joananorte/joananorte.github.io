Mobile app

Bridgewhat Matching

Helping companies find the right growth Advisors through tailored matching.

Role
UI/UX Designer

Year
2025

The problem

The matching process considers multiple dimensions—from geography and sector to functional expertise, use cases and growth themes. The experience needed to make this breadth feel clear and manageable, while helping users understand why an Advisor was being recommended.

Project vision

Bridgewhat Matching is a mobile experience designed to help companies identify and connect with experienced Advisors aligned with their growth needs. It brings search, filtering and a dedicated matching dashboard into one focused journey.

Design approach

I began with wireframes to establish the app’s structure, navigation and core flows. From there, I developed high-fidelity mockups for browsing matches, reviewing Advisor profiles and refining preferences.

Outcome

The final designs provide a focused, mobile-first matching experience that helps companies discover relevant Advisors and compare recommendations with confidence.
