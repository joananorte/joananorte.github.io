ScopeLanding page mockups · Visual direction

The brief

Create adaptable landing-page mockups for BMW and MINI—two distinct brand worlds that needed clear hierarchy, strong automotive storytelling and a conversion-focused path to the next action.

The outcome

A flexible set of visual concepts to guide website production, balancing product-led content, campaign messaging and modular layouts that can evolve with each brief.

Design approach

Two brands, two digital personalities.

01

Translate the brief

Each mockup starts from the campaign goal, audience and the content that needs to lead the experience.

02

Shape the hierarchy

Product information, imagery and calls to action are arranged to make the next step immediately clear.

03

Design for implementation

The outcome is a clear visual blueprint, ready to be developed into the live brand website.

Final design

Landing page directions

BMW — product-led landing page

Refined, spacious layouts that let the vehicle, technology and core message take the lead.

MINI — campaign-led landing page

A more expressive, playful direction that brings the campaign character into every interaction.
