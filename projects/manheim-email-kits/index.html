Email design system

Manheim Email Kits

Responsive email templates for auction, customer and commercial communications.

RoleUI/UX Designer
ScopeEmail Templates · Smarkio · HTML

The problem

Manheim needed a flexible collection of email templates for auction, customer communication and commercial campaigns — all clear, on-brand and ready to scale across channels.

Project vision

Build a modular email kit that works across desktop and mobile, with templates that can be configured in Smarkio or delivered as bespoke HTML when the communication requires it.

Design approach

I created a practical and consistent template system: define the email hierarchy, adapt each message to its audience and make sure the layout works across devices. Some journeys were configured in Smarkio, while others were developed directly in HTML.
