Insurance website

Chubb Website

A complete website redesign for a global insurance company, designed to make information clearer and services easier to explore.

RoleUX/UI Designer
Year2022

The problem

Insurance websites often bring together complex information, products and support journeys. The challenge was to make the experience feel clear, reassuring and simple to navigate for every visitor.

Project vision

Chubb Website is a mobile experience designed to help companies identify and connect with experienced Advisors aligned with their growth needs. It brings search, filtering and a dedicated matching dashboard into one focused journey.

Design approach

I mapped the website structure and key customer journeys before developing the visual direction. The redesigned system balances clarity, accessibility and an approachable insurance experience.
