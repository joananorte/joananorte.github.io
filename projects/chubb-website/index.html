Insurance website

Chubb Website

A complete website redesign for a global insurance company.

RoleUI/UX Designer
ScopeUI/UX Design · Website Redesign

The problem

Insurance websites often bring together complex information, products and support journeys. The challenge was to make the experience feel clear, reassuring and simple to navigate for every visitor.

Project vision

Chubb Website is a responsive redesign that makes insurance products, coverages and supporting information easier to explore. It brings clear hierarchy, reassuring visual cues and straightforward paths to the right solution.

Design approach

I mapped the website structure and key customer journeys before developing the visual direction. The redesigned system balances clarity, accessibility and an approachable insurance experience.
