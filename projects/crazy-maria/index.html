Virtual escape training

Crazy marIA

A virtual escape training to help teams learn more about Artificial Intelligence.

RoleUI/UX Designer & Front-end Developer
ScopeUI/UX Design · Front-end Development

The problem

Artificial Intelligence is transforming the workplace quickly, but many teams do not know where to start. The experience needed to make AI learning more approachable, engaging and practical for every participant.

Project vision

Crazy marIA is a virtual escape training that turns AI learning into an interactive challenge. Teams explore concepts, make decisions and collaborate while building a more confident relationship with Artificial Intelligence. Designed primarily for desktop learning in a workplace context, the experience remains fully responsive across devices.

Design approach

I began with wireframes to map the training flow, challenges and learning moments. From there, I designed the complete visual experience and built it in HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
