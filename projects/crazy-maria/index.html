The problem

AI is transforming the workplace quickly, but it can be difficult for teams to know where to begin. The training needed to make the subject less abstract, more approachable and genuinely engaging for employees.

Project vision

Crazy marIA turns AI learning into an interactive challenge. Through an escape-room format, participants explore concepts, make decisions and collaborate while building a more confident relationship with Artificial Intelligence.
