Virtual escape training

Crazy marIA

An interactive virtual escape training designed to help teams build practical awareness and confidence around Artificial Intelligence.

\n Visit landing page ↗
RoleUX/UI Designer & Front-end developer
ScopeWireframes · Design · HTML/CSS/JS

The problem

Artificial Intelligence is transforming the workplace quickly, but many teams do not know where to start. The experience needed to make AI learning more approachable, engaging and practical for every participant.

Project vision

Crazy marIA is a virtual escape training that turns AI learning into an interactive challenge. Teams explore concepts, make decisions and collaborate while building a more confident relationship with Artificial Intelligence.

Design approach

I began with wireframes to map the training flow, challenges and learning moments. From there, I designed the complete visual experience and built it in HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
