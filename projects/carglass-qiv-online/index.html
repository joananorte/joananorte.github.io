QIV Online

An evolving digital platform that helps insurance mediation teams handle isolated glass-breakage requests with greater clarity.

Interface previews to come

RoleUI/UX Designer
Year2025—2026

The challenge

QIV Online supports a service that sits between insurance mediation, policy information and vehicle-glass repair. The experience needs to make each request easy to understand and straightforward to progress.

Project vision

Rather than a fixed handover, this is an ongoing product. Each new request becomes an opportunity to refine flows, introduce new screens and make the platform more useful for the people who rely on it.

Design approach

I work iteratively with the client: clarifying each requirement, mapping the relevant journey and creating focused interface updates. This keeps the platform consistent while allowing it to evolve one practical need at a time.
