Digital platform

Carglass QIV Online

An evolving digital platform for isolated glass-breakage requests.

RoleUI/UX Designer
Year2025—2026

The problem

Requests related to isolated glass breakage involve policy information, mediation teams and repair services. The platform needs to make each journey clear and efficient.

Project vision

An ongoing product that evolves alongside the client’s needs. Each request is translated into a focused, consistent interface update.

Design approach

I work iteratively with the client: clarify the requirement, map the relevant flow and create the necessary screen or adjustment. This allows the product to grow without losing consistency.
