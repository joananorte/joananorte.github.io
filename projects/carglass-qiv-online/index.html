Digital platform

Carglass QIV Online

A digital platform for insurance intermediaries to manage and book vehicle-glass claims.

RoleUI/UX Designer
ScopeProduct Design · UI Evolution

The problem

Managing vehicle-glass claims involves policy information, insurance intermediaries and repair booking. QIV Online needed to make every step clear and efficient.

Project vision

A continuous product experience that gives mediators and brokers a clear path from claim management to repair booking, evolving through focused interface updates.

Design approach

I work iteratively with the client: clarify the requirement, map the relevant flow and create the necessary screen or adjustment. This allows the product to grow without losing consistency.
